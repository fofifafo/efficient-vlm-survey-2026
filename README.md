## Contents

### `data/` — corpus, taxonomy, screening, matrices (Sections 3–4)
- `corpus_vlm_15394.csv` — the 15,394 VLM-relevant arXiv papers (2024-01-01 to 2026-08-31): arXiv id, date, primary category, title, `efficiency_oriented` (1 = in the 1,947-paper efficiency pool) and, for each branch 4.1–4.6, `b4.x_loose` / `b4.x_strict` (rules v2.1, Section 3.2). Built by `code/build_corpus_csv.py`; its per-branch totals equal `taxonomy_counts.csv`. Abstracts are not redistributed; fetch them by arXiv id.
- `taxonomy_counts.csv` — yearly counts and shares, relative growth, Fisher and Cochran–Armitage p-values, conditional shares, per pool × branch × definition (Table 2, Fig. 2). `strict_sensitivity.csv` — strict-rule variants (Section 3.4). `branch_labels.csv` translates the Korean pool/branch labels used in these files.
- `screening/` — `screening_verdicts.csv`: all 618 screening-desk cards (475 papers) with verdict (`mark`: keep / star = representative / skip), candidate list (`b`, `R`/`R2` = second-round lists), `rec` (model "recommended" flag), `code` (code link detected), venue, citations, sub-branch (Korean) and title (Section 3.5). `tier` is the provisional desk grade; final grades are computed from the matrices by `code/paper_numbers.py`. `selected_all.csv` — the kept papers (70; Mini-InternVL, 2410.16261, kept at screening, was removed from the deployment branch after the second rater's eligibility review, Section 3.5, so the matrices hold 69 papers). The screening criteria are given in the supplementary material. `code_link_check.*`, `code_link_files.json` — automated code-link check (57 links).
- `matrices/` — `matrix_en.csv`: English comparison matrix behind Tables 3–7; `matrix_4.1.csv`–`matrix_4.5.csv`: working matrices (more columns: base models, accuracy, compared methods, code link, notes; partly Korean) from which the grades are derived (corrected on 21 October 2026, see below); `EXTRACTION_RUBRIC.md`: the 14 sentence patterns and extraction rules; `evidence/matrix_evidence_*.json|md`: candidate supporting sentences collected from each full text per pattern (`R` = second-round lists).
- `numbers/paper_numbers.json|md` — every number quoted in the paper with its source, including the evidence grades of the 71 matrix entries (69 papers; LLaVA-MoD appears in 4.1 and 4.3, SPEED-Q in 4.3 and 4.5): A 46, B 13, C 12; `tables/` — Tables 2–9 and the Jetson table as generated (file numbers follow the generator, not the final table numbering; the A† marks of Tables 3–7 are added in the manuscript and listed in `validation/grades/table_cell_corrections.json`).
- `robustness/` — `exec_rule_sensitivity.json` (execution-time branch under the base and a broader vocabulary: yearly counts, shares, growth, Fisher p, recall per year on the first rater's labels, detection of six influential methods, papers added by 'large multimodal model'/'LMM'; Section 3.2, Table S7; from `code/exec_rule_sensitivity.py`) `flag_sensitivity.json` (grades without the 26 papers that received a model 'recommended' flag; Section 3.5; from `code/flag_sensitivity.py`), `robustness.json` (decomposition of the execution-time growth into growth of the efficiency pool and growth within it, the abstract audit restricted to method-proposing abstracts, entries that do not measure the cost they claim to reduce, and the largest cross-GPU difference in speed-up size; Sections 3.4, 3.5, 4.6 and 5.3; from `code/robustness_numbers.py`, loaded by `paper_numbers.py` as `robust.*`) `audit_patterns.json` (the abstract-audit patterns of Section 4.6, written by `paper_numbers.py`) and `review_stats.json` (same-period comparison January–August 2024 vs 2026 with Bonferroni and Holm correction, the bootstrap ratio of the execution-time and compression growth factors, the misclassification-corrected growth, the within-budget ranks on the two GPUs, and paired bootstrap intervals of the 40 accuracy differences of Table S9; from `code/review_stats.py`).

### `Supplementary_Material.pdf`
The supplementary material of the paper (S1–S9: queries and rules, sensitivity, screening, classifier validation with Table S5 and the second rater, extraction rubric and grade correction, measurement details with Table S6 and the CDPruner diagnosis, use of language models, and robustness checks with Table S7 (broader execution-time vocabulary), the model-flag sensitivity, the claim-matched grade Table S8 (prefill memory against batch size), Table S9 (bootstrap intervals of the accuracy differences), Table S10 (comparison with selection without a score) and Table S11 (torch.compile)).

### `measurements/` — measurement study (Section 5, Tables 8–11, Figs. 4–5)
- `code/` — `harness.py` (TTFT, end-to-end latency, peak memory, NVML energy), `methods/*.py` (one plug-in per method, including the changes described in Section 5.1; `uniform.py` and `pool.py` are the baselines without a score of Section 5.2, `base_compile.py` the torch.compile condition of Section 5.3, and `fastv.py` has the settings `k2`/`k2r889` for the paper's layer 2), `eval_acc.py` + `score.py` (GQA, POPE, TextVQA, MME scorer), environment and baseline setup (`setup_env.sh`, `download.sh`, `pin_transformers.sh`, `clone_baselines.sh`, `build_fastv_kv.sh`, `fastv_env.sh`), runners (`run_baselines.sh`, `run_second_session.sh`, `run_base_alone.sh` and `run_compile.sh` for the runs of 28 October 2026), `make_table8.py`, `make_table_cross.py`, `jetson/` (Jetson harness, NF4 micro-benchmark, accuracy), `profiling/` (CDPruner stream diagnosis and TTFT profiling: `profile_ttft.py`, `run_profile_all.sh`, `rerun_base_profile.sh`, `profile_cdpruner_diag.py`, `run_cdpruner_diag.sh`, `_check_stream_pool.py`), `check_answer_length.py` (counts RTX 5080 answers that may have reached the 16-token generation cap; Table 9 accuracies). `commits.csv` — upstream commits of the five methods; `accuracy_reported.csv` — accuracies reported in the original papers (Table 10).
- `results_pc1/` — RTX 5080 machine: `latency_<setting>.json` per method (5 runs × 500 GQA questions, per-run statistics) and `acc_<setting>/` (`accuracy.json`, `meta.json`, per-question predictions `preds_*.jsonl`). `default` = paper's main setting, `r889` = 64 tokens (88.9 % removed). `uniform/` (evenly spaced 128 and 64 of the 576 projector outputs), `pool/` (2×2 and 3×3 average pooling, 144 and 64 tokens) and `fastv/*k2*` (FastV at layer 2) were measured in a second session on 28 October 2026 (Table 8). `FAILURES.md` and `sparsevlm/failed_unpatched_latency_default.json` record the SparseVLM run that peaked at 17.6 GB before the loader fix of Section 5.1.
- `results_pc2/` — RTX 5070 Ti replication (same layout): latency, TextVQA and all FastV results; environment in `env.txt`, `check_env.json`, `pc2_commits.txt`, `gpu.txt`. `results_pc2_accuracy/` — GQA, POPE and MME of the nine non-FastV settings on the RTX 5070 Ti under the RTX 5080 settings (up to 16 generated tokens, FP16 throughout, CDPruner fix), with predictions and logs; these are the RTX 5070 Ti accuracies of Table 9.
- `results_pc1_memory_batch/memory_batch.json` — prefill memory of LLaVA-1.5-7B against batch size 1–16 with 576 or 64 visual tokens on the RTX 5080 (Section 5.2, Table S8; `code/memory_batch.py`, `code/run_memory_batch.sh`). Peak and above-weights values are PyTorch allocations; the 576-token runs at batch size 8 and 16 exceed the card and completed only because the driver spilled into system memory, so their `seconds` are not meaningful.
- `results_pc1_second_session/` — the unmodified model (`base/`, and `base_alone/` run with nothing else on the machine) and the ten compressed settings of Table 8 re-measured in the session of the new baselines (Table 8 caption; within −4.4 to +0.9 % of Table 8). `results_pc1_compile/` — torch.compile on the unmodified model, eager / vision encoder / vision encoder and language model, one session, with logs (Section 5.3, Table S11). `baseline_comparison.json` — paired bootstrap comparisons of the methods with evenly spaced tokens at the same budget, FastV layer 2 vs 3, and the baselines vs the unmodified model (Section 5.2, Table S10; from `code/baseline_comparison.py`).
- `results_cross/table_cross.csv|md` — the two GPUs side by side (Tables 8–9); `results_jetson/` — Jetson Orin Nano (Table 11: `base4`, `vispruner4`, token sweep `sweep/`, `microbench_nf4.json`).
- `results_pc1_profile/` (RTX 5080) and `results_pc2_profile/` (RTX 5070 Ti) — TTFT split into GPU and host time, 17 October 2026 (Table 9 Host column, Table S6): `<method>_<setting>.json` (per-question `wall_ms`, `gpu_busy_ms`, `host_ms` and their medians over 95 GQA questions; 100 run, first five discarded) and `cdpruner_diag_<variant>_<setting>.json` (CDPruner memory and TTFT diagnosis, 45 questions). `results_pc1_profile/base_first_run/` keeps the first RTX 5080 unmodified-model run (133.6 ms TTFT), made while desktop applications held 1.2–2.3 GB of GPU memory; the reported row (113.9 ms) is the re-run with those applications closed (`rerun_base_profile.sh`). `results_pc1_profile/logs/` holds the RTX 5080 run logs; RTX 5070 Ti logs are not included.

### `validation/` — classifier, grade and extraction checks (Sections 3.2, 3.5; supplementary S5–S6)
- `classification/` — first-rater judgments exported without titles/abstracts: `round1_precision_rater1.csv` (300 rows, rules v2), `round1_recall_rater1.csv` (100), `round2_precision_rater1.csv` (55 rows, rules v2.1 re-check of 4.3/4.5); `answer_key_*.csv` (rule outputs and strata per row); `classval_result_rater1_only.json`, `round2_result.json` (precision with Wilson CIs; the κ fields of the former compare rater 1 with itself and are not meaningful); `scripts/` (sheet builders, scorers, `try_rule_fix.py` for the v2 → v2.1 rule change, `export_rater1_csv.py`). The scorers read the original .xlsx sheets, which are not released because they contain abstracts. `classval_table_current.json` (from `scripts/recall_current_rules.py`) gives per-branch precision and recall under the current rules v2.1 with Wilson CIs (supplementary Table S5), using only the first rater's existing judgments; for 4.3 and 4.5 recall comes from the same sample that was used to narrow the rules and may be optimistic.
- `second_rater/` — the independent second rater's judgments and answer keys, released now that rating is complete. `second_rater_classification.xlsx`: sheets A (60 rows of the round-1 precision sample), B (20 rows of the recall sample) and C (11 rows of the round-2 sample), returned 15 October 2026; the abstract column is blanked (fetch abstracts by arXiv id), titles are kept. `second_rater_grades.xlsx`: 20 matrix entries rated for code availability (Q1) and measured cost (Q2), with links and evidence locations, returned 20 October 2026. `key_second_rater.csv` maps each sheet row (`new_row`) to the first-rater sheet row or `answer_key_grade.csv` row (`orig_row`; sheet G = grades). `second_rater_result.json` (κ and agreement for sheets A, B, C), `second_rater_branch.json` (agreement per branch on sheet A), `second_rater_grade_result.json` (grade agreement and the six disagreements, computed against the grades before the 21 October correction), `second_rater_disagreements.xlsx` (the 19 classification disagreements). `scripts/`: sheet builders (`make_second_rater_sheet.py`, `make_grade_only_sheet.py`), scorers (`score_second_rater.py`, `score_grade_only.py`, `branch_breakdown.py`) and `check_r2_reliability.py` (blank or contradictory answers; whether the cited tables, units and numbers occur in the full text). The second rater worked without the first rater's answers and without AI tools. In the released copies, workbook file names referenced by the scripts are replaced by the names above.
- `grades/` — `answer_key_grade.csv` (code and measured-cost facts and the grade per matrix entry, after the 21 October correction) and the correction materials: `recheck_c_candidates.py` / `.md` (candidate lines with time or memory units from the full texts, appendices included, of the 18 grade-C papers outside the second rater's sample), `grade_recheck_evidence.json` (Korean and English cell text and the supporting sentence for each corrected entry); `table_cell_corrections.json` (the 26 October cell corrections with their supporting sentences, and the seven grade-A entries marked A† because the measured cost is not of the kind the paper claims to reduce).
- `extraction_check/` — 30-quantity sample (`sample.csv`), result, and scripts.

### `code/` — corpus and statistics

## Grade correction (21 October 2026)

The second rater's grade check (20 entries) found measured costs in 3 of the 5 sampled entries we had graded C: SWIFT (Table 4), MiniDrive (Table 2) and Eve (Table 5). We then re-read the full texts of the 18 grade-C papers outside that sample under the unchanged rule (time or memory measured by the authors on their own system, training time included; FLOPs, capacity statements and numbers cited from other papers excluded) and found measured costs in 8 more: FitPrune, CLIP-LoRA, LLaVA-MoD, DeepSeek-VL2, KRAdapter, MoSLoRA, Mirage and VLA-GSE. Kimi-VL stays C (a relative throughput statement without values or conditions). In total 12 matrix entries (11 papers; LLaVA-MoD counts in 4.1 and 4.3) received measured costs. The original extraction missed them for two reasons: candidate sentences were collected only up to the references, so measurements in appendices were not seen, and training-time and memory tables whose units appear only in table cells were not matched. The LLaVA-MoD hardware was also corrected from H800 (a value for another paper's model, quoted in its introduction) to A100. Grades of the 71 entries changed from A 36 / B 11 / C 24 to A 46 / B 13 / C 12. Changed rows carry a `10/21` note in `matrix_4.*.csv`; supporting sentences are in `validation/grades/grade_recheck_evidence.json`; `paper_numbers.json|md` and Tables 3–7 were regenerated with `code/paper_numbers.py`.

## Matrix provenance

The CSV files in `data/matrices/` are the authoritative data; the matrices were built from the evidence sentences and then corrected by hand-checked edits, which are recorded here rather than as scripts. Corrections, in order: 16 October audit (FastV and VideoChat-Flash latency; five memory cells that stated a device capacity or an estimate removed; QA-IG quantization time 0.58 vs 0.55 GPU h for MBQ); 21 October grade correction (below; supporting sentences in `validation/grades/grade_recheck_evidence.json`); 25 October wording (the time column of Tables 3–7 is headed "Time"; the VLA-GSE cell reads "values only in a figure of the paper"); 26 October cell values (measured values copied from the papers into six cells that pointed to a table or named the model loosely — DART FLOPs and KV cache, CDPruner model name, TDA test time, EdgeVLA latency and memory — and three cells normalized to n/s or "in a figure"; supporting sentences in `validation/grades/table_cell_corrections.json`); 26 October review corrections (SparseVLM reduction 576→192 is 66.7%, not 77.8%; the FastVLM and NanoVLA memory cells, a model-size and a device-capacity statement, emptied because they are not measurements, so 32 rather than 34 entries report memory; grades unchanged). Changed rows carry a dated note in `matrix_4.*.csv`.

## Reproducing

CSV files are UTF-8 with a byte-order mark (so that Excel shows the Korean text correctly); read them with `encoding="utf-8-sig"`.


The scripts are released as used and expect the original project layout: `code/*.py` at the project root; `data/taxonomy_counts.csv`, `data/strict_sensitivity.csv`, `data/matrices/matrix_*.csv` and `data/screening/code_link_*` in `main_survey/`; `data/screening/screening_verdicts.csv` as `screening_marks_export.csv` at the root; `validation/classification/round2_result.json`, `classval_table_current.json` and `scripts/recall_current_rules.py` in `main_survey/classval/`; `validation/grades/*` in `main_survey/gradeval/`; `data/robustness/*` and `measurements/baseline_comparison.json` in `main_survey/` (`audit_patterns.json` in `draft/numbers/`); `validation/second_rater/*` (scripts included) in `main_survey/second_rater/`; `measurements/code/*` (including the files in `profiling/`), `measurements/results_*` and `accuracy_reported.csv` in `bench/`; outputs go to `draft/numbers/` and `draft/tables/`.

**Table 2 and Fig. 2 (counts).**
1. `pip install arxiv && python arxiv_scrape.py` rebuilds the arXiv cache `papers_raw.json` (about 30 MB with abstracts, about 15 min; not redistributed). The arXiv API can return slightly different records later (withdrawn or re-dated papers); `data/corpus_vlm_15394.csv` fixes the id list used in the paper.
2. `python main_taxonomy.py` → `main_survey/taxonomy_counts.csv` (15,394 VLM papers, 1,947 efficiency-oriented; Table 2). `python strict_sensitivity.py` → strict-rule variants.
3. `python paper_numbers.py` → `draft/numbers/paper_numbers.json`, Tables 2–9; `python make_figures.py` → Figs. 2, 4 and 5 (needs matplotlib). Fig. 1 and Fig. 3 are TikZ drawings in the manuscript source.
4. `python build_corpus_csv.py --cache papers_raw.json` regenerates the corpus CSV and prints per-branch totals for comparison with `taxonomy_counts.csv`.

**Tables 8–11 (measurements).** WSL2 Ubuntu with a 16 GB NVIDIA GPU. `bash setup_env.sh` → `bash download.sh` (LLaVA-1.5-7B, CLIP ViT-L/14-336, GQA, POPE, TextVQA, MME from Hugging Face, about 30 GB) → `bash pin_transformers.sh` → `bash clone_baselines.sh` (then check out the commits in `commits.csv`) → `bash build_fastv_kv.sh`. Latency: `bash run_harness.sh <method> 500 5 <default|r889>` with `<method>` ∈ {base, vispruner, cdpruner, cdpruner_fix, sparsevlm, dart, fastv, uniform, pool} (FastV also `k2`, `k2r889`; torch.compile: `python harness.py --method base_compile --setting <vision|full>`, or `bash run_compile.sh 500 5`); accuracy: `bash run_acc.sh <method> <setting>` (or `python eval_acc.py --method <m> --setting <s> --tasks gqa,pope,mme --max_new_tokens 16 --out <dir>`, as used for `results_pc2_accuracy/`); batch memory: `bash run_memory_batch.sh` (`python memory_batch.py --bs 1,2,4,8,16`). Then `python make_table8.py` and `python make_table_cross.py`. Jetson: `jetson/export_gqa500.py`, `jetson/reshard_llava.py`, `jetson/harness_jetson.py`, `jetson/acc_jetson.py`, `jetson/microbench_nf4.py`. Paths default to `~/vlmbench` (`WORK`) and the script directory (`BENCH`).

**Table 9 Host column and Table S6 (TTFT split).** With the GPU otherwise idle, `bash run_profile_all.sh` (`N=100`) runs a 10-question smoke test and then `python profile_ttft.py --method <m> --setting <default|r889> --n 100` for the 11 settings (FastV uses `~/venv_fastv` if present). For each of the first 100 questions of the harness GQA sample (seed 20261001, `max_new_tokens=1`), the script measures TTFT without the profiler (`wall_ms`) and then reruns the same question under `torch.profiler` (CUDA activity only) to obtain the union of kernel and copy intervals (`gpu_busy_ms`); `host_ms` = wall − busy. The first five questions are discarded and medians are reported. Output goes to `~/vlmbench/results_profile/<method>_<setting>.json` and is copied here as `results_pc1_profile/` and `results_pc2_profile/`. The Host column of Table 9 is `host_ms` (RTX 5080 / RTX 5070 Ti); Table S6 lists `wall_ms`, `gpu_busy_ms` and `host_ms` for both GPUs.

**CDPruner diagnosis.** `bash run_cdpruner_diag.sh` (`N=50`) runs `profile_cdpruner_diag.py` for the released CDPruner (128 and 64 tokens) and the default-stream fix (128 tokens), one variant per process. It records GPU memory before and after loading and every 10 questions (allocated, reserved, free, allocation retries, nvidia-smi usage), the TTFT of 45 questions after five warm-up questions, and the CLIP tower time on the default stream and through the wrapper. The released `CLIPVisionTower.forward` creates two CUDA side streams per call; `torch.cuda.Stream()` hands out streams from a pool of 32 per device and the caching allocator keeps freed blocks per stream, so about 1.5 GB of extra cached memory accumulates and a 16 GB GPU is left with almost no free memory. Under WSL2, TTFT then becomes slow and erratic, with no allocation retries or out-of-memory errors. Median TTFT, released vs fixed at 128 tokens: 103 vs 78 ms on the RTX 5080, 284 vs 74 ms on the RTX 5070 Ti. `_check_stream_pool.py` reproduces the stream-pool and cached-memory behaviour without a model. Reported upstream as https://github.com/Theia-4869/CDPruner/issues/24 (17 October 2026; CDPruner commit 9541616).

**Second rater and grade check.** In `main_survey/second_rater/`: `python score_second_rater.py second_rater_classification.xlsx <first-rater round-2 sheet>` → `second_rater_result.json` and `second_rater_disagreements.xlsx`; `python score_grade_only.py second_rater_grades.xlsx` → `second_rater_grade_result.json`; `python branch_breakdown.py second_rater_classification.xlsx` → `second_rater_branch.json`. The first-rater comparisons read the first rater's original sheets (`classval/returned_*.xlsx`, not released because they contain abstracts; the judgments themselves are in `validation/classification/*_rater1.csv`). `recheck_c_candidates.py` and `check_r2_reliability.py` read the full texts in `main_survey/txt/` (not redistributed; see `code/fetch_pdfs.py`).

**Hardware and software.** PC1: RTX 5080 16 GB, Core Ultra 5 245KF. PC2: RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB (driver 591.86), Ryzen 9 9950X3D. Both Windows 11 + WSL2 (PC2: Ubuntu 24.04.4, kernel 6.6.87.2, `.wslconfig` memory 24 GB / swap 16 GB); PyTorch 2.11.0+cu128, CUDA 12.8, transformers 4.37.2, accelerate 0.27.2, tokenizers 0.15.1; LLaVA-1.5-7B FP16, SDPA attention, `vicuna_v1` template, batch size 1, greedy. Jetson Orin Nano Developer Kit 8 GB, JetPack 6.2, MAXN SUPER, PyTorch 2.11.0, language model in 4-bit NF4 (bitsandbytes). Logs contain local home-directory paths.
